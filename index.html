Sizing the problem

$26.6B

Estimated annual US cost of menopause symptoms, medical expenses plus lost work time. A national estimate, not what any one employer pays.

Source: Mayo Clinic Proceedings, 2023.

$216M vs $1.68B

Women's and maternal health spend when claims are grouped by specialty versus by condition.

Source: Purple Labs commercial claims, 2024, descriptive, not matched.

175 days

Median time from a woman's first symptom claim to the diagnosis that explains it. One in four women under 40 waits more than a year.

Source: Purple Labs.

Why doesn't menopause show up as a cost in claims?

Symptoms are billed separately under the specialties that treat them: sleep, anxiety, joint pain and bleeding. The underlying cause often goes uncoded. One report found that only 14.7% of women aged 45 to 64 had evidence of menopause in their medical record.
