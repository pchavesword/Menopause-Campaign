Frequently
asked questions

What is the hidden cost of women’s health?

Spend scattered across claims that never adds up to one number. One woman’s care can span primary care, behavioral health, orthopedics, neurology, gynecology and physical therapy, and none of it is labeled menopause. Not all of it is avoidable. The opportunity is to see where better care can make a measurable difference.

Why doesn’t menopause show up as a cost in claims?

Symptoms are billed separately under the specialties that treat them: sleep, anxiety, joint pain and bleeding. The underlying cause often goes uncoded. One report found that only 14.7% of women aged 45 to 64 had evidence of menopause in their medical record.

What results has Sword Women’s Health measured?

In a matched claims study of 42,516 commercial members: $2,484 saved per participating member per year, 3.2x gross ROI and surgical encounters down 65%. Medical claims only. Milliman reviewed the methodology only. It did not audit the data, validate savings or recalculate results.

How should employers evaluate women’s health vendors?

Test against four measures: time to diagnosis, avoidable ER use, specialty coordination and site of care. Then ask five questions about payment, risk and independent review. The guide includes an RFP insert.
